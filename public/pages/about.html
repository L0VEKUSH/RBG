Our History

Founded in 1995, Sunrise Public School began with a small vision to provide quality education in the local community. Over the last 25+ years, we have grown into a premier institution.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To empower every student to achieve their full potential and become responsible, global citizens.

Mission: Providing holistic education through modern teaching methodologies, instilling moral values, and promoting excellence in sports and arts.
